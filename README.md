Mount Taygetus

Mount Taygetus is a fast-paced 2D vertical platformer developed for the ITEC Gamejam 2026. Scale the heights of the legendary peak, master movement abilities from fellow climbers, and manage your resources to secure the fastest time.
🏔️ The Journey

Ascend through vibrant pixel-art environments where every jump counts. Along the way, you will encounter unique NPCs who grant you the powers needed to reach the summit:

  Dash: Quick horizontal bursts to bridge wide gaps.

  Double Jump: Gain extra height to reach soaring ledges.

  Grappling Hook: Swing across dangerous terrain with precision.

🪙 Token Mechanics: Risk vs. Reward

The core of Mount Taygetus is the Token System. Balancing your survival with your final score is the key to mastery.

  The Penalty: If your token count hits zero, the mountain wins, your run is reset.

  The Bonus: Every token you hold when you cross the finish line shaves 2 seconds off your total time.

  Strategy Tip: Do you spend tokens to safely navigate obstacles, or hoard them to climb the speedrun leaderboard?
